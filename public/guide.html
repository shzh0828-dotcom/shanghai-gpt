What is included

21 selectable destinations; historic Bund façades; four distinctive landmark towers; five cultural destinations; five shopping destinations; the convention center; a steel truss bridge; supporting city blocks, promenade details, trees and lights; seven moving boats and 72 vehicles; over 2,000 mapped building outlines and 2,000 road/path sections.

The export contains buildings, ground, river surface, bridge, vegetation, façade details, boats, and vehicles. Instanced details are expanded to ordinary meshes for compatibility. It captures a static moment; water motion, traffic routes, tours, labels, sky, and lighting presets remain in the browser. Add lighting in Blender or use Material Preview.

Geography & modeling notes

This is an artistic, procedural miniature, not survey-grade mapping. East is positive X and north is negative Z. The geographic base uses OpenStreetMap roads, building outlines, riverbanks, and green spaces at a uniform scale of 0.22 model units per metre, centered on 121.492° E, 31.236° N. Mapped building heights or floor counts are used where available; missing heights are estimated. Individually modeled landmarks have interpreted façades and approximate footprints. Major destinations stay on the correct riverbank with approximate relative positions. Nanjing Road extends inland; the BFC/Fosun complex sits south of the historic Bund.

The Bund History Museum is represented by the memorial above its location in Huangpu Park. Museum interiors are omitted. The former HSBC building and the Pudong Development Bank building are one landmark. IFC and BFC are simplified complexes. The model uses architectural interpretations, not exact façade reproductions. Water uses lightweight moving geometry, not physically accurate reflections. No live traffic, opening hours, or exhibition listings are represented.
