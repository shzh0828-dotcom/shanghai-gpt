# Photo references

User-provided photos are retained here as modeling references only, not embedded in the published app. They do not establish a license for reuse elsewhere.

1. Lujiazui: Shanghai World Financial Center (left), Jin Mao Tower (middle), Shanghai Tower (right).
2. Pudong skyline from the Bund: Oriental Pearl, International Convention Center with spherical features in the foreground, and the Lujiazui high-rise cluster.
3. Historic Bund: former HSBC Building with its central dome, Customs House with its clock tower. Supporting rear buildings are separate from the waterfront façades.
4. Bund at night: former HSBC Building, Customs House, and Peace Hotel with a green pyramidal roof further along the waterfront.
