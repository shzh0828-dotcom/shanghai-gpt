# Landmark exterior reference pass

The miniature uses 0.22 scene units per metre for published heights. It is a reference-based exterior reconstruction, not a surveyed BIM model. Footprints and setback details retain the site's compressed city layout. Small trim, glazing, roof equipment, and unmeasured heights remain approximations. Photographic colors are interpreted as neutral material colors; day/sunset/night light is applied separately.

## Published dimensions used

- Shanghai Tower: 632 m, 120-degree twisting envelope, nine vertical neighborhoods, recessed crown. [Gensler](https://www.gensler.com/projects/shanghai-tower)
- SWFC: 492 m, 101 floors, square-prism/arc concept and open upper portal. [Owner](https://swfc-shanghai.com/about_intro.php?l=en), [KPF](https://www.kpf.com/project/shanghai-world-financial-center). Width and arc curvature are photo-derived, not surveyed.
- Jin Mao: 420.5 m, 88 floors; stepped steel/glass curtain wall and aluminium fins. Removes the former artistic height increase. [Facade contractor](https://www.permasteelisagroup.com/historic-project/jin-mao-tower/)
- Oriental Pearl: 468 m. [Shanghai tourism](https://spanish.shanghai.gov.cn/sp-%20PlacestoGo/20240729/2d1517571fcf4d69ab7170b227808158.html). Pearl positions and support geometry are photo-derived approximations.
- IFC: 260/250 m main towers, 85 m third tower, four-level podium, sculpted corners. [Architect](https://pcparch.com/work/shanghai-ifc), [KONE](https://www.kone.cn/en/stories-and-references/references/international-finance-center.aspx)
- BFC: two 180 m towers, stone/bronze details and lower waterfront blocks. [Foster + Partners](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/bund-finance-center?altTemplate=ProjectPDF)
- Customs House: 79.2 m total; corrects an earlier roughly 90 m miniature. [Xinhua reporting from the building's anniversary](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/2017-12/23/c_1122156734.htm)
- Peace Hotel: 77 m; green copper pyramid and stepped sandstone facade. [Shanghai government](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw5827/20200905/0001-5827_632318.html)
- Museum of Art Pudong: 30 m, warm-white granite, river-facing Great Glass. [Shanghai tourism](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/pudong-new-area/museum-of-art-pudong-193151/), [Jean Nouvel](https://www.jeannouvel.com/en/projects/shanghai-pudong-art-museum/)
- Convention Centre: 40 m main body, unequal glass spheres 50/38 m in diameter. Model's overall height follows the larger sphere. [Design institute](https://www.ziad.cn/product/13.html)
- Aurora Plaza: retained surveyed footprint and 185 m height. [Glass supplier](https://www.taiwanglass.com/application_detail.php?id=527&langeno=en&sid=159). The existing I-heart-SH screen is user-requested miniature content, not the real building's fixed design.
- Waibaidu Bridge: 104.39 m deck length and approximately 18.4 m width, two spans. [Bridge reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waibaidu_Bridge). Truss height/detail is interpreted from photographs.
- People's Heroes Monument: 60 m; three angular granite shafts replacing the previous small tripod. [Monument reference](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/上海市人民英雄紀念塔)

## Exterior references with estimated dimensions

- Former HSBC: retained limestone banking facade, colonnade, drum and shallow dome; retained approximate height. [Historic photographic archive](https://hpcbristol.net/visual/ro-n0172)
- Bank of China: vertical stone piers and restrained Chinese roof profile. [Bank's own history](https://www.boc.cn/aboutboc/ab7/200809/t20080926_6924.html). Height approximately 75 m for this miniature, not verified from this source.
- Rockbund Art Museum: six-floor Art Deco structure, vertical bays and recessed roof terrace. [David Chipperfield](https://davidchipperfield.com/projects/rockbund-project). Approximately 30 m model height.
- Bund 18: six-floor classical facade, columns and cornices. [Restoration architect](https://kokaistudios.com/zh/portfolio/bund-18.html). Approximately 32 m model height.
- Shanghai Club: pale classical facade and paired corner roof pavilions. [Hilton historical material](https://www.hilton.com.cn/file/pdf_doc/file_20190821114701279sE24QY1.pdf). Approximately 32 m model height.
- Fosun: layered bronze veil, 675 tassels on three tracks. [Heatherwick](https://heatherwick.com/project/bund-finance-centre/). Approximately 30 m model height; the veil is static in this miniature.
- Aurora Museum: six-floor glass envelope and circular stair volume. [Museum](https://www.auroramuseum.cn/en/about-us). Approximately 30 m model height.
- Super Brand Mall: layered retail volume, glazing and facade bands. [JERDE](https://www.jerde.com/projects/8354/super-brand-mall), [Mall](https://www.superbrandmall.com/en/about-us). Retained approximate height.
- Nanjing Road: a streetscape rather than a single building. Existing retail blocks receive differentiated stone facades, windows and cornices; not a surveyed reconstruction of every shop.
